# Helios Dynamics Employee Handbook (2026 edition)

## 1. Working hours and remote work

Standard working hours are 09:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing probation. Remote work
requires a stable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps and use of the company VPN at all times.
Teams must agree on two "anchor days" per week when everyone is in the office.

## 2. Probation

All new employees serve a probation period of 90 days. During probation the notice period is
7 days. After confirmation the notice period becomes 60 days for individual contributors and
90 days for managers.

## 3. Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at
2 days per month. Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over to the next year; any
remaining days lapse on 31 March. Employees also receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
Sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

Parental leave: primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary
caregivers receive 4 weeks. Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## 4. Expenses

Business meals are reimbursed up to $60 per person per day. Expense claims must be submitted
through the Ledger portal within 30 days with an itemised receipt. Claims above $500 need
written approval from a department head before the expense is incurred.

## 5. Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and conferences.
Unused learning budget does not carry over.

Helios HX-200 Home Battery - Support FAQ

What is the capacity of the HX-200?
The HX-200 stores 12.5 kWh of usable energy and delivers up to 5 kW continuous power (7.6 kW peak).

What warranty does the HX-200 have?
The HX-200 has a 10-year warranty or 6,000 full cycles, whichever comes first. The warranty
guarantees at least 70% of original capacity at the end of the warranty period.

What does error code E-417 mean?
E-417 means a thermal sensor fault. Switch the unit off at the isolator, wait 10 minutes, and turn
it back on. If E-417 returns within 24 hours, book a technician visit.

What does error code E-102 mean?
E-102 means the grid connection was lost. The battery switches to backup mode automatically and
returns to normal operation when the grid is restored.

Which firmware version is current?
The current firmware is version 4.2.1, released in September 2026. It improves charge scheduling
during off-peak tariffs. Updates install automatically overnight.

Can the HX-200 be installed outdoors?
Yes. The HX-200 is rated IP65 and operates between -10 C and 50 C. It must not be installed in
direct sunlight on south-facing walls.

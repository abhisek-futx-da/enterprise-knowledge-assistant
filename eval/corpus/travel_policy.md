# Helios Dynamics Business Travel Policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the Wayfarer travel portal at least 14 days before
departure. Bookings made later than this need approval from a department head.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for
flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires approval from the CFO.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night in standard cities and $320 per night in high-cost
cities (London, New York, Tokyo, Zurich and Singapore).

## Per diem

Travellers receive a per diem of $75 per day for meals and incidentals instead of submitting meal
receipts. The per diem replaces the daily business-meal reimbursement while travelling.

## Ground transport

Use public transport or ride-sharing where practical. Car rental requires a business reason and
is limited to compact or mid-size vehicles.
